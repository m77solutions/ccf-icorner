The Problem We Hear Every Sunday

At the Welcome Center, inquirers consistently ask three questions:

  1. "Where do I start?"
  2. "Is there a Couples Retreat / GLC / Men's Fellowship coming up?"
  3. "How do I sign up?"

Today: Volunteers answer from memory, printed flyers, or phone screens — often with outdated info.

The cost: Inquirers walk away with incomplete next steps. Ministries lose first-touch momentum.
